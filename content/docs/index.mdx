## Our Vision

NVNM Chain is a purpose-built Layer 2 blockchain for accountability infrastructure in agentic workflows.

Designed for institutional and regulated environments, it separates verification from data exposure — anchoring cryptographic proof of what agents did without the underlying data ever leaving its lawful custodian.

## NVNM Chain is The Verifiable Data Layer for Private Markets

General-purpose blockchains can store a hash, but they lack the agent identity, role-based access controls, and typed access surface that multi-party agentic workflows require. NVNM Chain is purpose-built for that gap: a dedicated substrate for anchoring cryptographic proofs of what AI agents saw, computed, and acted upon — across any data platform whose operators need their counterparties to verify the work independently, without a vendor sitting in the middle as the arbiter of truth.

- Anchors hashes, evidence URIs, and compliance events
- Designed for high-frequency institutional data workflows
- Enables agentic AI systems to verify and interact with private asset data
- Creates a tamper-evident record of every consequential action an AI agent takes
- Maintains independent runtime and governance
