mantraUSD is the deposit and gas asset for NVNM Chain. This page covers every supported path to get mantraUSD into your wallet on MANTRA Chain.

---

## Option 1 — Express Bridge (get.mantrausd.com)

The fastest and simplest route. The [mantraUSD Express Bridge](https://get.mantrausd.com) accepts USDC or USDT from Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and Arbitrum and delivers mantraUSD to your MANTRA Chain wallet at a 1:1 ratio with no slippage.

This is the recommended path if you are starting from stablecoins on a major EVM chain.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [get.mantrausd.com](https://get.mantrausd.com)
2. Connect your wallet on the source chain
3. Select your source asset (USDC or USDT) and source chain
4. Enter the amount and confirm the transaction
5. mantraUSD arrives on MANTRA Chain at 1:1

---

## Option 2 — Squid Router (squidrouter.com)

[Squid Router](https://squidrouter.com) is a cross-chain swap aggregator. It routes from any supported asset on any supported chain directly into mantraUSD on MANTRA Chain in a single transaction.

Use this path if you are starting from a non-stablecoin asset (ETH, BTC, USDC on an unsupported chain, etc.) or if you want a single-step cross-chain swap without manual bridging.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [Squid Router](https://app.squidrouter.com/?fromChain=arbitrum&toChain=mantra&fromToken=eth&toToken=mantrausd)
2. Set the destination chain to MANTRA Chain and the destination token to mantraUSD
3. Select your source chain and asset
4. Review the route and confirm

## Option 3 — Bridge USDT via Lucid, then swap on QuickSwap

This path uses Lucid to bridge USDT to MANTRA Chain, then swaps to mantraUSD on QuickSwap.

**Step 1 — Bridge USDT via Lucid**

Go to [Lucid](https://app.lucidlabs.fi/bridge) and bridge USDT from your source chain to MANTRA Chain. Lucid delivers native USDT (`0x3806640578b710d8480910bF51510bc538d2F51A`) to your MANTRA Chain EVM wallet.

**Step 2 — Swap USDT for mantraUSD on QuickSwap**

Go to [QuickSwap](https://dapp.quickswap.exchange/swap?type=v4&from=0x3806640578b710d8480910bF51510bc538d2F51A&to=0xd2b95283011E47257917770D28Bb3EE44c849f6F&chainId=5888) on MANTRA Chain (Chain ID 5888). The link preloads USDT as the source and mantraUSD as the destination. Confirm the swap.

> **Note:** You need a small MANTRA balance in your wallet to pay gas on MANTRA Chain. See [Acquire Tokens](../getting-started/acquire-tokens) if your wallet has no MANTRA for gas.

## Option 4 — Bridge USDC via Hyperlane, then swap on QuickSwap

This path uses Hyperlane to bridge USDC to MANTRA Chain, then swaps to mantraUSD on QuickSwap.

**Step 1 — Bridge USDC via Hyperlane**

Go to [Hyperlane Nexus](https://nexus.hyperlane.xyz/?destination=mantra&destinationToken=USDC) and bridge USDC from your source chain to MANTRA Chain. The link preloads MANTRA Chain as the destination with USDC as the destination token. Hyperlane delivers bridged USDC (`0x5E76be0F4e09057D75140216F70fd4cE3365bb29`) to your MANTRA Chain EVM wallet.

**Step 2 — Swap USDC for mantraUSD on QuickSwap**

Go to [QuickSwap](https://dapp.quickswap.exchange/swap?type=v4&from=0x5E76be0F4e09057D75140216F70fd4cE3365bb29&to=0xd2b95283011E47257917770D28Bb3EE44c849f6F&chainId=5888) on MANTRA Chain (Chain ID 5888). The link preloads USDC as the source and mantraUSD as the destination. Confirm the swap.

> **Note:** You need a small MANTRA balance in your wallet to pay gas on MANTRA Chain. See [Acquire Tokens](../getting-started/acquire-tokens) if your wallet has no MANTRA for gas.

## Which path should I use?

| Starting point | Recommended path |
|---|---|
| USDC or USDT on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum | Option 1 — Express Bridge |
| Any asset on any supported chain | Option 2 — Squid Router |
| USDT on another chain, prefer manual bridge | Option 3 — Lucid + QuickSwap |
| USDC on another chain, prefer manual bridge | Option 4 — Hyperlane + QuickSwap |
