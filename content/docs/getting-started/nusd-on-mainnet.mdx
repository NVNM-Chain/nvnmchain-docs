# nUSD on Mainnet

nUSD is the fee token on NVNM Chain Mainnet. Every transaction pays gas in nUSD, so you need an nUSD balance before you can send your first transaction.

<Warning title="Mainnet details pending">
  NVNM Chain L1 Mainnet has not launched yet. The supported ways to get nUSD on Mainnet will be published here before launch. Unlike [Testnet](/getting-started/nusd-faucet-testnet) and [Devnet](/developer-guides/pathusd-faucet-devnet), Mainnet has **no faucet**: nUSD on Mainnet carries real value.
</Warning>

## Check your balance

NVNM Chain has no native token, so the balance your wallet shows next to nUSD is a placeholder. To read your real nUSD balance, query the nUSD TIP-20 contract. See [Token balances and fees](/developer-guides/token-balances-and-fees) for the contract address and example code.

<Note>
  The nUSD contract address on Mainnet is listed on [Network Details](/resources/network-details) once it is published.
</Note>
