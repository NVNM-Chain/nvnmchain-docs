---
title: "How to Setup Your Keplr Wallet"
icon: "wallet"
---

This guide walks you through setting up Keplr Wallet and connecting it to MANTRA Chain for both testnet and mainnet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Keplr Wallet">
    Navigate to [https://keplr.app/](https://keplr.app/) and install the Keplr Wallet extension compatible with your browser. Keplr Wallet is essential for managing your crypto assets on the Cosmos ecosystem.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/connect-mainnet/keplr-install.png" alt="Keplr Wallet Installation" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Keplr Wallet">
    Add a wallet name, set up a password, and secure your seed phrase or recovery phrase.

    <Warning>
      **Never** share your recovery phrase with anyone.
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/connect-mainnet/keplr-setup.png" alt="Keplr Wallet Setup" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add MANTRA Chain to Keplr">
    Visit the official MANTRA Chain Explorer at [https://explorer.mantrachain.io](https://explorer.mantrachain.io/). Select the applicable chain and use the **Wallet Tools** to import the chain connection details into the wallet of your choosing (Keplr, Leap, and MetaMask are supported).

    Click **Approve** to ensure seamless integration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Manage Chain Visibility">
    Open your Keplr Wallet extension and navigate to the **Manage Chain Visibility** section. From here, add MANTRA Chain to your wallet.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/connect-mainnet/keplr-manage-chains.png" alt="Keplr Manage Chain Visibility" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add MANTRA DuKong Testnet">
    Search for **MANTRA** in the list of available chains and check the box next to **MANTRA DuKong Testnet** to access testnet features for development purposes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy Your MANTRA Address">
    Grab your MANTRA address from Keplr Wallet via the MANTRA token address. This address serves as your unique identifier on the MANTRA network and is essential for receiving tokens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request Testnet Coins from Faucet">
    To get testnet tokens:

    1. Navigate to the [MANTRA Dukong Faucet](https://faucet.dukong.mantrachain.io/)
    2. Ensure **mantra-dukong-1** is selected as the network
    3. Enter your MANTRA wallet address or EVM wallet address
    4. Click **Request Token** — the testnet tokens will be sent to your wallet
  </Step>
</Steps>
