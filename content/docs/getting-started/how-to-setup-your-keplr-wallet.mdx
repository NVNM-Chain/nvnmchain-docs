This guide walks you through installing Keplr Wallet and adding the NVNM Chain network.

<Note>
  Keplr is the recommended Cosmos wallet for NVNM Chain. It allows you to interact with NVNM Chain and the broader Cosmos ecosystem natively.
</Note>

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Keplr">
    Navigate to [https://keplr.app](https://keplr.app) and install the Keplr extension for your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, and Edge are supported).

    <Warning>
      Only install Keplr from the official site at [keplr.app](https://keplr.app) or your browser's extension store. Avoid third-party download links.
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/connect-mainnet/keplr-install.png" alt="Keplr Wallet Installation" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create or import a wallet">
    Open the Keplr extension and choose one of the following:

    - **Create a new wallet** — Follow the prompts to set a wallet name, password, and back up your recovery phrase.
    - **Import an existing wallet** — Enter your 12- or 24-word recovery phrase, or import via private key or hardware wallet.

    <Warning>
      **Never** share your recovery phrase or private key with anyone. Anyone with access can control your wallet and funds.
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/connect-mainnet/keplr-setup.png" alt="Keplr Wallet Setup" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add NVNM Testnet to Keplr

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Chain Visibility">
    Click the **menu** button in the Keplr extension and select **Manage Chain Visibility** to open the chain browser.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for NVNM Testnet">
    Use the search bar to look for `nvnm testnet`. Check the box next to **NVNM Testnet** to enable it in your wallet.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/connect-testnet/keplr-add-nvnm-testnet.png" alt="Keplr Add NVNM Testnet" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and switch to NVNM Testnet">
    Click **Save** to add the network. Select **NVNM Testnet** from the chain selector. Your wallet is now connected to the NVNM Chain testnet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add NVNM Mainnet to Keplr

<Warning>
  NVNM Chain mainnet has not launched yet. This section will be updated once mainnet is live and the chain is registered with Keplr.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Chain Visibility">
    Click the **menu** button in the Keplr extension and select **Manage Chain Visibility** to open the chain browser.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for NVNM Mainnet">
    Use the search bar to look for `nvnm`. Check the box next to **NVNM Mainnet** to enable it in your wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and switch to NVNM Mainnet">
    Click **Save** to add the network. Select **NVNM Mainnet** from the chain selector. Your wallet is now connected to NVNM Chain mainnet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Once NVNM Chain mainnet launches, follow the [connect wallets to mainnet](/docs/getting-started/how-to-connect-wallets-to-mainnet) guide to add the mainnet network.
</Tip>
