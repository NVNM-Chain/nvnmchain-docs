This guide walks you through installing Keplr Wallet and adding the NVNM Chain network.

<Note>
  Keplr is the recommended Cosmos wallet for NVNM Chain. It allows you to interact with NVNM Chain and the broader Cosmos ecosystem natively.
</Note>

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Keplr">
    1. Visit [https://keplr.app](https://keplr.app) and click the download button for **Chrome**. You will be directed to the Chrome Web Store.

        <Frame>
          ![Keplr website download for Chrome button](/images/keplrsetup/chromedownload.png)
        </Frame>

    2. Click **Add to Chrome**.

        <Frame>
          ![Google Chrome add Keplr extension](/images/keplrsetup/chromeadd.png)
        </Frame>

    3. On the pop up, click **Add extension**.

        <Frame>
          ![Google Chrome add Keplr extension confirm](/images/keplrsetup/extensionadd.png)
        </Frame>

    4. After adding the Keplr extension, Keplr will automatically open. You can also make sure it's easily accessible in your toolbar by clicking the jigsaw icon in the top-right of the screen, and hitting the pin icon.

        <Frame>
          ![Google Chrome pin Keplr extension](/images/keplrsetup/extensionpin.png)
        </Frame>

    <Warning>
      Only install Keplr from the official site at [keplr.app](https://keplr.app) or your browser's extension store. Avoid third-party download links.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create or import a wallet">
    Open the Keplr extension and choose one of the following:

    - **Create a new wallet** — Follow the prompts to set a wallet name, password, and back up your recovery phrase.
    - **Import an existing wallet** — Enter your 12- or 24-word recovery phrase, or import via private key or hardware wallet.

    <Warning>
      **Never** share your recovery phrase or private key with anyone. Anyone with access can control your wallet and funds.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add NVNM Testnet to Keplr

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Chain Visibility">
    1. Click the **menu** icon (three lines) in the top-right of the Keplr extension.

        <Frame>
          ![Keplr extension menu button](/images/keplrsetup/hitsettings.png)
        </Frame>

    2. Select **Add/Remove Chains** to open the chain browser.

        <Frame>
          ![Keplr Add/Remove Chains menu option](/images/keplrsetup/hitadd-removechains.png)
        </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for NVNM Testnet">
    Use the search bar to look for `nvnm testnet`. Check the box next to **NVNM Testnet** to enable it in your wallet.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting NVNM Testnet in Keplr](/images/keplrsetup/selecttestnet.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
